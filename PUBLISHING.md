publisher explicitly approves making the repository public and publishing it.

and all release asset hashes. No private QA footage or research is uploaded.

third-party corresponding-source ZIP. Do not describe this as anonymity verified.

folder, compare SHA256SUMS.txt and never upload the qa, build, source-archives,
